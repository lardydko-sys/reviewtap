Frequently asked questions

Where do I find my Google review link?
Open the Google Maps app, tap your profile picture → “Your business profile” → “Ask for reviews” → “Copy link”. Paste it into ReviewTap during setup or in Settings → Business info.

Does ReviewTap send texts automatically?
No. ReviewTap opens your phone’s Messages, WhatsApp or Mail app with the message filled in. You check it and tap Send yourself.

Where is my customer list stored?
Only on your iPhone. We never upload your customers’ names, phone numbers or emails. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the iPhone Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions → ReviewTap → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

I got a new iPhone. How do I get Pro back?
Open ReviewTap → Settings → Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Is it OK to ask customers for reviews?
Yes. Google allows businesses to ask for reviews. Google does not allow offering rewards for reviews or asking only happy customers. ReviewTap is designed to help you ask everyone, fairly.
