Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ReviewTap iPhone app (“ReviewTap”, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. ReviewTap is operated by Ilarion Dudko. In short: your business and customer information stays on your iPhone. We do not sell data, we do not show ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

1. Information that stays on your device

The following information is stored only in the app’s storage on your iPhone. We never upload it to our servers:

  • Your business name, Google review link and message templates.
  • The customer names, phone numbers and email addresses you enter, and the history of review requests you sent.

If you delete the app, this information is deleted from your device. It may be included in your own iCloud or iTunes device backups, which are controlled by Apple and you.

2. Messages you send

When you send a review request, ReviewTap opens your phone’s Messages, Mail or WhatsApp app (or copies the text) with the message filled in. You choose whether to send it. Those messages are delivered by Apple, your mobile carrier, your email provider or WhatsApp under their own privacy policies. ReviewTap does not receive or store the content of your conversations.

You are responsible for having permission to contact your customers and for following the laws that apply to business messages in your area.

3. Anonymous usage analytics

To improve the app, ReviewTap may send anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, PostHog, Inc. (servers in the United States). Examples: “onboarding completed”, “review request sent (by text, in Spanish)”, “paywall viewed”. These events include basic technical information such as app version, device model and operating system version, and a random identifier created by the app. They never include your customers’ names, phone numbers, email addresses, your business name or message text. We do not use this data to identify you, for advertising or for tracking.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment card details. To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives a random app user ID, your purchase receipts and basic device information. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Notifications

If you turn on follow-up reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone. They are created on your device and are not sent through our servers.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location or microphone.

7. Data retention and your choices

You can edit or delete any customer in the app at any time, or delete the app to remove all locally stored data. Anonymous analytics events are kept for up to 12 months. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device’s random identifier, or for any privacy question, email lardydko@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Because we hold almost no personal information, most requests can be completed in the app itself. We will answer any request within 30 days.

8. Children

ReviewTap is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date above. Important changes will also be noted in the app’s update notes.
